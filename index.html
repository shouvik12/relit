Relit

The 1993 Doom engine, relit for today

id Software's 1993 engine runs the game, with its rules unchanged. A new renderer draws it in real 3D, with art remastered by AI on your own GPU: free mouselook, a flashlight with real shadows, lights that move with every fireball, sparks, smoke and embers, and 3D sound with the original music.

The Slipgate Complex, Castle of the Damned, the Necropolis and the Grisly Grotto, rebuilt as Doom maps from John Romero's GPL map source, with Doom's monsters, weapons and textures. Needs a Doom 1-style file such as freedoom1.wad.

Or drop a .wad file here. Got Relit from GitHub? Pick game/freedoom1.wad. Otherwise download Freedoom free, unzip it and pick freedoom1.wad. Your own doom.wad or doom2.wad works too. The file never leaves your computer.
