Wrecklight's driveable cars Ironhide, Quicksilver and Old Thunder come from
Stunt Rally 3 (https://github.com/stuntrally/stuntrally3), converted to
maps/cars/*.rcar by tools/stuntrally-car. Relit adds the roof gun, rocket
pods, ram bars and lamps.
